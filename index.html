Power Score blends regular-season record (50%), points scored (30%), and points differential (20%) into one number, each scaled against the rest of the league that season, so it reflects who actually played well — not just who got favorable matchups. Draft data covers 2012–2025 · roster expanded to 16 rounds in 2021–2023 due to COVID-era squad size increases
2012–2024 ran on NFL.com, which forced the first N rounds of the draft to be keeper selections (N = that season's keeper count). So for those years, "keeper" means "was picked in one of the first N rounds," not an explicit flag — the round counts themselves come from the league's own records, not inferred. 2025 onward uses explicit, individually-confirmed keeper picks instead, since keeper rounds became flexible that year. Player's NFL team also isn't shown — we only ever captured fantasy team names in the draft data, not real-world NFL teams, and pulling a player's current team would be wrong for older picks.
